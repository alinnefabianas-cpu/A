# Description
This woman survived the Titanic. And that wasn't even the craziest part. 🚢

Violet Jessop worked on ships as a stewardess. In 1911, she was on the Olympic, Titanic's sister ship, when it collided with a warship. In 1912, she survived the Titanic on a lifeboat. Then in 1916, she was on the Britannic, the third sister ship, when it hit a mine and sank, and she jumped into the sea to escape the propellers.

She survived all three. People called her "Miss Unsinkable".
