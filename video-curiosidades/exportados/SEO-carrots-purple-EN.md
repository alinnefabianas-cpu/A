# Title
Carrots Used to Be PURPLE 🥕💜

# Description
Carrots used to be purple. 💜
The first farmed carrots were mostly purple and yellow. In the 1600s, Dutch farmers made orange carrots popular. People love to say it was a tribute to William of Orange, but there's no real proof of that.

Did you know this? 👇
