# Description
Napoleon wasn't short. The whole world got tricked by a measuring mistake. 📏

His height was recorded as 5 feet 2 inches, but in French units, and a French inch was about 7% longer than an English one. Converted, he was roughly 5'6"–5'7" (about 1.69 m), average or even a little tall for a Frenchman of his time. The "tiny tyrant" image came from British cartoonists, a nickname of affection ("the little corporal") and the tall guards who usually stood around him.
