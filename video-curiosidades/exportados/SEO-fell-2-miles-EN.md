# Description
This girl fell two miles out of the sky. And survived. 😳

On Christmas Eve 1971, 17-year-old Juliane Koepcke was flying over the Amazon rainforest when lightning hit the plane and it broke apart in midair. She fell about 3,000 meters, still strapped to her seat, and crashed into the jungle trees.

She was the only survivor. Alone, with a broken collarbone and just one shoe, she remembered what her biologist parents taught her: follow the water downstream. After 11 days, she reached a hut and workers found her.
