# Description
Every time you use Bluetooth, you're saying the name of a Viking king. 👑

Over a thousand years ago, Harald "Bluetooth" Gormsson ruled Denmark and was famous for uniting Denmark and Norway. Historians think his nickname may come from a dead tooth that looked dark blue. In the 1990s, engineers from rival tech companies needed a code name for a wireless standard, and after talking about a book on Vikings, one of them suggested "Bluetooth": like the king, it would unite things that didn't get along. It was meant to be temporary, but it stuck. And the logo? Harald's initials in Viking runes.

Did you know this? 👇
