# Description
Bubble wrap was supposed to be wallpaper. 🫧

In 1957, two engineers in New Jersey sealed two plastic shower curtains together, trapping air bubbles inside. Their big idea: fancy, textured wallpaper. Nobody wanted it. Greenhouse insulation flopped too. Then, in 1961, a computer company needed a way to ship its fragile new machines, and those little air bubbles were perfect.
