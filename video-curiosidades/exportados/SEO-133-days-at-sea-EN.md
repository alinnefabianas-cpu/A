# Description
This man survived 133 days alone in the middle of the ocean, on a tiny wooden raft. 🌊

In 1942, Poon Lim was working on a British cargo ship when a German submarine torpedoed it. The ship sank in minutes and he found an empty life raft. When the biscuits and water ran out, he made a fishing hook from the wire in his flashlight, drank rainwater and caught seabirds with his bare hands. Ships passed him by, a plane spotted him… and then a storm hit.

On day 133, fishermen off the coast of Brazil finally rescued him. He walked off the boat on his own.
