# Description
Why are airplane windows round? Because sharp corners can rip a plane apart. ✈️

In 1952, the world's first jet airliner flew with big, nearly square windows. In 1954, two of them broke apart in mid-air. Investigators sank an entire plane in a giant water tank and pressurized it again and again, and found metal fatigue: every flight the cabin swells like a balloon, and that stress piles up at sharp corners of windows and openings until cracks form. Rounded windows spread the stress out, so there's no weak point.
