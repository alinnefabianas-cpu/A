# Description
Ketchup used to be sold as medicine. Seriously. 💊🍅

Early ketchups weren't even made from tomatoes: some were made from mushrooms, others from fish. In the 1830s, Ohio doctor John Cook Bennett claimed tomatoes could treat things like diarrhea and indigestion, and turned tomato ketchup into pills. He teamed up with a drug maker and tomato pills became a huge craze. Copycats rushed in promising to cure almost anything, but some of their pills didn't contain any tomato at all; they were just laxatives. By around 1850, the craze had faded away.
