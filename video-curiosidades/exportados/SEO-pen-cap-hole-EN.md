# Title
The Hole in Your Pen Cap Could SAVE A LIFE 🖊️

# Description
That little hole in your pen cap could save a life. 🖊️
Lots of people chew on pen caps, especially kids, and sometimes a cap gets swallowed by accident. If it gets stuck in the throat, the hole can let a little air pass through. That's why many pen makers put a hole in the cap on purpose.

Did you know this? 👇
