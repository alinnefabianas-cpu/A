# Description
The Mona Lisa wasn't the most famous painting in the world... until someone stole it. 🖼️

In 1911 it was just one painting among thousands in the Louvre. Then, one Monday morning, a handyman who had worked at the museum walked out with it hidden under his smock, and nobody noticed until the next day (the staff assumed it was being photographed). The theft became front-page news around the world, and people even lined up to stare at the empty spot on the wall. Two years later the thief tried to sell it in Florence and got caught. By the time it came back to Paris, the Mona Lisa was the most famous painting on Earth.
