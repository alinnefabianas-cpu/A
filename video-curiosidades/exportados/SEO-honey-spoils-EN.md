# Title
Honey Basically NEVER Goes Bad 🍯

# Description
Honey basically never goes bad. 🍯
It has very little water, so germs can't grow in it. It's also naturally acidic, and bees add an enzyme that makes a tiny bit of hydrogen peroxide. Archaeologists have reportedly found pots of honey in ancient Egyptian tombs, thousands of years old, and still edible!

If your honey gets hard and cloudy, don't throw it away. It's just crystallized. Warm it up!
