# Title
Why Your Fingers WRINKLE in Water 🛁

# Description
Your fingers don't wrinkle because they soak up water. 🛁
It's actually your nervous system doing it on purpose: your nerves tell tiny blood vessels under the skin to shrink, and the skin above pulls in. Doctors noticed that if the nerve to a finger is damaged, that finger doesn't wrinkle at all. Scientists think the wrinkles might help us grip wet things, like tire treads!
