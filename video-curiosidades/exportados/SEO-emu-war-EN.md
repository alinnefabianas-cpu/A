# Description
In 1932, Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight emus. The emus won. 🐦

After World War One, veterans were given farmland in Western Australia, until around 20,000 emus showed up and ate the wheat. The army sent in machine guns, but the emus ran too fast, scattered too quickly, and even outran a truck. After weeks and thousands of bullets, the army pulled out. History calls it the Great Emu War.
