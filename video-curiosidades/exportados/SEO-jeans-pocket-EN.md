# Title
The Tiny Jeans Pocket Has a SECRET Job 👖

# Description
That tiny pocket on your jeans has a secret job. 👖
It goes back to the 1800s, when workers wore jeans every day and many men carried pocket watches, not phones. A watch could break or get lost in a big pocket, so jeans got a little pocket just the right size for a watch. Today people use it for coins, keys or guitar picks, but it's still called the watch pocket.
