# Description
The 1904 Olympic marathon in St. Louis might be the craziest race ever run. 🏃

Scorching heat, dusty roads and only one water stop. The first man across the line had ridden in a car for eleven miles. The real winner, Thomas Hicks, was kept going by his trainers with strychnine (rat poison) and brandy, then used as a stimulant. A Cuban mail carrier ran in his street clothes and still finished fourth, another runner was chased off course by wild dogs, and only 14 of 32 starters finished.

Which part is the wildest? 👇
