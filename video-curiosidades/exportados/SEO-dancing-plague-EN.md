# Description
In 1518, people in Strasbourg started dancing... and couldn't stop. 💃

It began with one woman dancing in the street for days. Within a week dozens had joined her, and some accounts say it grew to hundreds. The city's doctors blamed "hot blood" and prescribed more dancing: they cleared halls, built a stage and paid musicians. It only made things worse. Dancers collapsed from exhaustion, and some later accounts claim people died, though the city's records are unclear. Many historians now think it was a stress-driven mass hysteria after years of famine and disease.
