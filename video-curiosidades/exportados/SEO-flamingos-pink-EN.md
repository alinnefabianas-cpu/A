# Title
Flamingos Are NOT Born Pink 🦩

# Description
Flamingos are not born pink. 🦩
Baby flamingos are grey and fluffy. Their pink color comes from their food: algae and tiny shrimp packed with natural pigments. The more they eat, the pinker they get, and if they stop, their feathers slowly fade. That's why zoos add special pigments to their food!
