# Title
Lost in the Sahara for 9 Days… and Survived 🏜️

# Description
This man got lost in the Sahara desert for nine days. And survived. 🏜️

In 1994, Mauro Prosperi was running a famous ultramarathon across the Sahara when a huge sandstorm erased the trail. With almost no water left, he drank his own urine, found an empty shrine and survived by eating the bats inside. After nine days, nomads found him… in Algeria, almost 300 km off course. And years later, he went back and ran the race again!
