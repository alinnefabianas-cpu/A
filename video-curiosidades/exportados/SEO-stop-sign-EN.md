# Description
Stop signs have eight sides for a reason, and it's not just to look cool. 🛑

The first stop signs showed up in Detroit around 1915: plain white with black letters. In the early 1920s, highway engineers came up with a rule: the more sides a sign has, the more danger it warns about. A circle (endless sides) went to railroad crossings, the most dangerous spot of all, and the octagon came next, so it became the stop sign. Its shape is so unique that drivers recognize it from far away, or even from behind. Plot twist: early stop signs weren't red. They were yellow, because red paint faded too fast in the sun. They officially turned red in 1954.
