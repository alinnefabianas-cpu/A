# Title
The Eiffel Tower Gets TALLER in Summer 🗼☀️

# Description
The Eiffel Tower gets taller in the summer. ☀️
It's made of iron, and iron expands when it heats up: on hot days the tower can grow by up to about 15 cm (6 inches), then shrink back in winter. And it was only supposed to stand for 20 years. It was saved partly because it made a perfect giant radio antenna.
