# Title
Pisa Was Leaning Before It Was Even FINISHED 😳

# Description
The Leaning Tower of Pisa started leaning before it was even finished. Construction began in 1173 on soft, sandy ground, and it was tilting by the third floor. Wars paused construction for about a century, and that pause probably saved it. In 1990 it was closed, and engineers straightened it… just a little.
