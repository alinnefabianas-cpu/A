# Title
Her Body Temperature Hit 13.7°C… and She Survived 🥶

# Description
Her body temperature dropped to 13.7 degrees Celsius. And she survived. 🥶

In 1999, Anna Bågenholm was skiing in Norway when she fell headfirst through the ice of a frozen stream. She breathed in an air pocket for 40 minutes, then her heart stopped. After 80 minutes trapped, rescuers pulled her out at 13.7°C (normal is 37). Doctors slowly warmed her blood with a machine for hours, and her heart started beating again. She later worked as a doctor at the same hospital that saved her.
