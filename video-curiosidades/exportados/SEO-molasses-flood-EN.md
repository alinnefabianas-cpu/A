# Description
In 1919, a giant wave of molasses flooded the streets of Boston. 🌊

On January 15, 1919, a huge tank holding over two million gallons of molasses burst open. A sticky wave up to 25 feet tall rushed through the North End at about 35 miles per hour. It crushed buildings, bent the steel of an elevated railway, and took 21 lives. The cleanup took weeks.

The worst part? The tank had leaked for years, and it was painted brown so the leaks would blend in. Locals claimed the neighborhood smelled sweet on hot days for decades.
