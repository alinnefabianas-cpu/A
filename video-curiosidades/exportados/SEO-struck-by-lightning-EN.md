# Description
This man was struck by lightning seven times. And he survived every single one. ⚡

Roy Sullivan was a park ranger in Virginia. Lightning burned off his toenail, his eyebrows, hit his shoulder, set his hair on fire (twice!), struck his ankle… and in 1977 it hit him a seventh time while he was fishing. Right after that, a bear reportedly tried to steal his fish!

He still holds the world record for the most lightning strikes survived.
