# Description
School buses are yellow because of one professor and a wall full of paint. 🚌

In the 1930s, school buses in America came in all kinds of colors and shapes. In 1939, professor Frank Cyr brought experts together to set national standards. He hung about fifty paint strips on a wall, from lemon yellow to deep orange-red, and they picked one special yellow: black letters on it are easy to read at dawn and dusk, and it's hard for drivers to miss, even out of the corner of their eye. Funny thing: there's no federal law that says school buses must be yellow. It just stuck, for more than eighty years.
