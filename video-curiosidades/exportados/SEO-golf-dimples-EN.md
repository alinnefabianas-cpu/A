# Title
Why Golf Balls Have DIMPLES ⛳

# Description
Golf balls have dimples for a surprising reason. ⛳
Early golf balls were smooth, but players noticed that old, beat-up balls flew farther than new ones. The dents change the air around the ball, so there's less drag. Today, a dimpled ball can fly about twice as far as a smooth one.
