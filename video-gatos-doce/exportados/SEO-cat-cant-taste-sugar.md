🐾 QUICK FACTS
• The sweet receptor is built from two genes. In cats, one of them (Tas1r2) is broken, so sweet can't be detected.
• Tigers and cheetahs have the same broken gene.
• Cats are tuned to umami, the savory taste of meat. Tuna is packed with the molecules that trigger it.
• Cats have about 470 taste buds. Humans have around 9,000.
• Many adult cats are lactose intolerant, so ice cream is not a good treat.
