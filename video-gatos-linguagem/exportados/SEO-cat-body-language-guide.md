🐾 QUICK GUIDE
• Tail up = hello. Question-mark tip = playful. Puffed = scared. Low or tucked = nervous. Swishing hard = irritated.
• Ears forward = relaxed. Turning = listening. Sideways ("airplane ears") = irritated. Flat back = fear.
• Slow blink = trust. You can slow blink back.
• Belly up = trust, but not always an invitation to rub (the belly trap).
• Purring usually means contentment, but cats also purr when stressed or in pain.
• Signs of overstimulation: tail twitching, skin rippling, ears turning sideways, purring stops. Stop petting.
