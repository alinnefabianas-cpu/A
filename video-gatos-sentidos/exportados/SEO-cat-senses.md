🐾 QUICK FACTS
• Cats can see in light about six times dimmer than we need, thanks to the tapetum lucidum (the reason their eyes glow).
• They likely see blues and greens well, while reds look dull. Detail is blurrier than ours, but motion detection is excellent.
• Cats hear up to about 64,000 Hz (we stop around 20,000 Hz), so they can hear ultrasonic squeaks of mice.
• A study found cats recognize their owner's voice, even if they don't always come.
• The "stinky face" after sniffing is the flehmen response: scent is sent to the vomeronasal organ.
• Cats have whiskers on the back of their front legs, and they sweat through their paw pads.
