🐾 QUICK FACTS
• In 1987, vets in New York studied 132 cats that fell from buildings. About 90% survived.
• Injuries got worse up to about the 7th floor, then started to drop.
• A falling cat reaches top speed (around 60 mph) after about 5 floors. A person reaches roughly double.
• Cats that died in the fall were rarely taken to the vet, so the study mostly saw survivors.

⚠️ Cats don't have nine lives. If you live in an apartment, window screens save lives.

📚 SOURCES
• Whitney & Mehlhaff (1987). "High-rise syndrome in cats." Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association, 191(11).
