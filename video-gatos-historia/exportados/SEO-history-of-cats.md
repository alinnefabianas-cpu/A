🐾 QUICK FACTS
• Every pet cat descends from the African wildcat (Felis lybica) of the Near East and North Africa.
• Cats likely "domesticated themselves" by hunting mice around the first farms.
• The oldest evidence: a ~9,500-year-old grave in Cyprus where a person was buried with a cat.
• In Egypt, cats were linked to the goddess Bastet, and hundreds of thousands of cat mummies have been found.
• Cats spread on ships along trade routes; ancient cat remains were even found at a Viking trading port.
• In 1963, Félicette became the only cat to fly to space. Larry has lived at 10 Downing Street since 2011.
• The first big cat show was held at the Crystal Palace, London, in 1871. The Siamese comes from Thailand (Siam).
